# The Last Ferry chapter plan

The voyage has 40 scenes across 13 chapters. It starts with short crossings and isolated hazards, then asks the keeper to carry those skills into moving transfers, overlapping hazards, and the storm beacon.

| Chapters | Learning and escalation |
| --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Teach ledge grabs, skiffs, and breakable boards at the landing. Introduce spikes and the hound separately on the quay. Meet the swarm and spider, then bait three strikes from the giant snake. Pass the rock-throwing monkey, then take shelter on a stump while the log rolls past. |
| 4–5 | Teach the two-chamber lock, tilting bridge, and solo steam vent at the pumpworks. Wait for an acid discharge to stop before crossing the drain. Add a trolley, cable drum, and sinking cargo net in the freightyard. |
| 6–8 | Approach the tethered boar on foot, dodge the monkey and pursuing hound, then jump five spaced spikes before dismounting at the Engine Galleries. Follow solo vents with a vent and spike bed. Run under a lifting roof and board a skiff directly from a tilting bridge. Run from the elephant and cross four loose planks with a swarm behind you. |
| 9–10 | Cross six faster floating decks, then climb through light bridges, shutters, and cages into longer jumps. Retrieve the ladder key, jump from a tilting bridge to a skiff, and practice the acid gate and stepped ascent in daylight. |
| 11–12 | Use the gorilla’s chest display to get a head start over five loose planks and a gap. The gorilla swings after you on ropes, then follows you into The Gull Roost, where you climb past a monkey. Clear the skiff channel at the start of Storm Glass. In The Night Watch, escape the swarm and hound, time two shutters, and pass between two monkeys that throw from opposite sides. |
| 13 | Run under a lifting roof between acid discharges. Jump a charging hound, five loose planks, and a long gap. Take the widest bridge-to-skiff jump. Cross six loose planks with a swarm behind you, then climb past a rock-throwing monkey to reach the light. |

The eight retired chapters were formerly numbered 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12, 15, and 17. Continue uses stable scene addresses and advances to the next retained entrance if the saved scene was retired.
