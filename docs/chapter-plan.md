# The eight-chapter voyage

Version 1.5 selects 94 of the earlier voyage’s 150 locations. The former freight and railway districts share one chapter, and the lighthouse ascent closes the storm crossing. All eight chapters are open for debugging.

| Chapter | Locations | Footing | What changes for the player |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| I · The Landing | 12 | Solid tarred timber with iron straps | Learn ledge catches and test the longer jumps before the hound turns for its second pass. |
| II · Mangrove Canopy | 11 | Grassy root mats and branches | Cross vines and broken limbs while insects follow through the canopy. |
| III · The Sluice House | 12 | Blue-grey masonry | Raise the lock pontoon, balance on floats, and follow spillways. |
| IV · The Upper Sidings | 12 | Riveted silver girders | Move ballast and board a waiting trolley. It stops on the far platform. |
| V · Boar Run | 12 | Red clay over charcoal | Approach the boar on foot. It runs automatically after boarding. Jump the washouts and dismount at the end of each stretch. |
| VI · Lantern Ridge | 11 | Jagged limestone shelves | Climb the ridge and find the next foothold in the keeper’s lamp light. |
| VII · The Lens Works | 12 | Sea-green leaded glass | Restore light bridges and pass through moving shutters. |
| VIII · The Storm Beacon | 12 | Pale cloud banks with scalloped undersides | Memorize gaps during lightning reveals, then signal the ferry from above the storm. |

Chapter materials identify ordinary ground. Working mechanisms, fragile planks and vines keep recognizable silhouettes. The main voyage has seven chamber transfers. Once aboard chamber I, tapping the hand carries the keeper to chamber II.

## Field guide for the playable build

| Creature | Where to find it | Behavior and player response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harbor hound | The Tarred Slip, chapter I | A recorded bark warns of its charge. Jump the first pass. It pauses at a post, turns, and runs back at the same speed. |
| Giant spider | Mangrove Canopy, with later encounters in the yard and galleries | Watch its eight-legged silhouette near the ground. Jump over its low body. It patrols between two limits at 110 pixels per second in either direction. |
| Mosquito swarm | Mangrove Canopy, locations 5 and 9 | The insects lift behind the keeper and fly toward his upper body at 42 pixels per second. They follow over gaps and elevation changes until the location ends. Keep moving once they stir. Contact sends the keeper back to the checkpoint. |
| Boar | All twelve locations of Boar Run | The boar takes off along the charcoal road. Left eases the pace, right urges it on, and jump clears a cut in the road. The keeper dismounts at checkpoint lamps. |
