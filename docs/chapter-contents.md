## Component catalog

Counts come from playable Godot layouts. The component columns include their use inside combinations. One net crossing and one drum encounter count as one each. Boar rides count scenes on the same animal. A gorilla chase counts once per encounter. Crates on balance platforms are included in the crate total.

The roof in The Sump Bridge moves vertically. Wait for it to rise, then run through before it comes down. The Overflow Lip drain glows green just before it discharges lethal acid. Cross between discharges. The Blind Landing and The Winding Floor combine both hazards. Leave the passage before the roof drops or the acid starts.
