# ⛴️ The Last Ferry chapter contents

**21 chapters · 94 playable scenes · 21 unique landscapes**

## 🧰 Component catalog

[Component image index](component-images/00-component-index.png) shows all 40 component types.

Counts come from the layouts loaded by Godot. A transfer drains the first chamber and fills the second. The keeper jumps between their floats. A hound counts once per encounter, a mosquito swarm counts as one swarm, and a two-chamber lock counts as one pair. The same boar carries the keeper through all 12 boar scenes during the campaign. The tables count those scenes. Trolleys are counted by mounted stretch. Crates used by balance mechanisms are included in the crate total.

Checkpoint stations can host a puzzle. Boar checkpoints save automatically during the ride. The campaign selects puzzles as you play, so the station count is a measure of placements.

Tilting bridges can reach about 34° and pull the keeper downhill. Both ends require a jump. Wide causeways use brass-edged blocks to signal longer jumps.

Dedicated gap, ledge, and drop counts cover their own sections. Rides, loose platforms, and other sections also have jumps. Routine connecting ground between challenges is part of the scene layout.
